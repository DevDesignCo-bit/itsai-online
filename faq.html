FAQ

What is ItsAi?

ItsAi is a subscription-based service that provides a platform for creating AI-powered PowerPoint presentations in seconds.

How does the subscription work?

Users can subscribe to ItsAi on a daily or weekly basis by confirming the subscription on the ItsAi mobile site. The subscription grants access to the platform, enabling users to create AI-infused PowerPoint presentations effortlessly.

What are the benefits of ItsAi?

Subscribers gain access to a powerful platform that leverages artificial intelligence to simplify and enhance the process of creating engaging PowerPoint presentations. ItsAi helps users save time and effort while producing high-quality presentations.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscribers can cancel their ItsAi subscription at any time by accessing their personal menu and clicking on the "Unsubscribe" link. The cancellation will prevent auto-renewal, and users will maintain access until the end of the current subscription period.

Can I try ItsAi before subscribing?

Currently, ItsAi does not offer a free trial. However, users can explore the platform’s features and benefits by subscribing for a daily or weekly plan.

Is my personal data secure with ItsAi?

ItsAi takes user privacy seriously. Personal data is handled in accordance with the Data Protection Act 1998. Users have the right to access, rectify, and/or oppose any personal data by contacting the data controller at NEXT MOVE DIGITAL.

Can I keep my PPTs if I unsubscribe?

You will retain the PowerPoint presentations that you downloaded, but you won’t have access to edit them on our platform if you unsubscribe.
